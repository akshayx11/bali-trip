Pure Veg Dining & Island Logistics

🥗 Featured Pure Veg Dining

Bollywood Masala Pure Veg Restaurant (Seminyak): Enjoy authentic North Indian vegetarian curries, dal makhani, paneer tikka, and tandoori rotis. Ubud also boasts world-famous vegan warungs (Warung Sopa, Moksa, Alchemy, Sayuri Healing Food).

🚤 SANUR SPEEDBOAT HARBOR

Modern Sanur Harbor pier handles fast boats to Nusa Penida (Banjar Nyuh). Boarding is smooth via gangways. Take motion sickness pills 30 mins before boarding if sea swells are high.

🚗 GRAB / GOJEK & DRIVER TIPS

Use Grab/Gojek apps in Seminyak and Kuta. For Ubud day tours (Lempuyang, Besakih, Bedugul), hire a reliable private driver (approx. 500k–650k IDR/day).

⛩️ SACRED TEMPLE RULES

Sarong & waist sash (selendang) are mandatory when entering Hindu temple compounds (available for rent/borrow at entrance). Wear modest shoulder-covering tops. Never step on Canang Sari floral sidewalk offerings.
